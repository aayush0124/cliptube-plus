      Supported platforms & limits

      Support depends on the installed yt-dlp version and what each site exposes for this URL. YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, and many other sites have extractors; an extractor does not guarantee every video can be saved.

      Available resolutions and audio tracks come from the source. Some formats require FFmpeg to combine video and audio. Estimated sizes may change during download.

      Login-only, private, DRM-protected, paywalled, and otherwise restricted media are outside this app’s public-media workflow. This app does not import browser cookies or collect sign-in details.
